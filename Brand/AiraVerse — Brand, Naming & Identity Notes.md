## 1. Persona

- **Name:** Aira
- **Pronunciation:** **"Eye-Ra"** (/ˈaɪrə/) — NOT "AIR-uh". This is deliberate.
- **Why the name "Aira" (confirmed by the user):**
  - **AI + Ra** → the name literally begins with **AI** (she is an AI persona), and **"Ra"** = the sun god = radiance / light. So "Aira" = **a radiant AI, a being of light** — which mirrors the luminous, glowing, light-driven signature of the reels.
  - **Also because she's super cute** — the name is short, soft, two clean syllables, warm off the tongue; it suits her personality.
  - Net: **cute on the surface, radiant underneath** — a rare combo of meaningful + adorable.

## 2. Brand Name — Final Decision

- **FINAL (display / ID name): "Aira's Verse"** — an evolution of the earlier "AiraVerse." The possessive **'s** reads warmer, more personal, and still means *"the verse that belongs to Aira."*
- **Handle: `hey.aira`** (see §6). The "hey" lives only in the @handle and the voice/bio; the brand still *reads* as "Aira's Verse."
- **Meaning/logic:** "the verse/universe of Aira" — literally what the reels are: a whole world she moves through (fantasy worlds, transformations, celestial/elemental realms, travel, beauty). The name *describes the project*.
- **Why it won the user's criteria:**
  1. Her name **"Aira" is written in full** and instantly readable (a hard requirement — see §3).
  2. **Catchy and easy to say:** "eye-ra's verse," one smooth breath.
  3. **Distinct + ownable** → protects against the "someone else takes the name" fear (see §4). "Verse" is far less contested than "Nova" (see §5).
  4. **Descriptive** of the content itself.
- **Rejected pivot:** **Aira Nova** — dropped because it collides with an existing **"Aria Nova"** AI-persona brand (one transposed letter, same niche). See §5.

## 3. The User's Naming Criteria (use these for any future naming)

1. **Aira must be written in FULL** and clearly readable — no blends that chop/hide her name.
2. **Catchy** and **easy to say out loud** (rolls off the tongue).
3. **Distinct / ownable** (coined or rare enough that a random account can't clash with it).
4. **Preserve the "Eye-Ra" pronunciation** — any attached word must not distort it.
5. **No "lux."** (See §5 for why.)

---

## 4. The "Someone else takes the name" fear — resolution (for reassurance if it recurs)

- A common first name can't be owned; a **distinct brand can**. That's why we attach a word to "Aira."
- **Real value of the project = the locked persona identity + the body of ~100 cinematic reels + visual signature + audience.** None of that transfers to a stranger using the word "Aira."
- **Protection plan:** (1) secure the handle everywhere NOW (possession ≈ ownership on social), (2) consistent name/logo/look across platforms, (3) build the body of work (recognition = the moat), (4) trademark later when it's worth it.
- **Dead lookalike accounts are not competition** — empty profiles have zero brand equity; real, consistent content makes you unmistakably the original.
