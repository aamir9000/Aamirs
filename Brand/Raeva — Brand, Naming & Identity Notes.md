## 1. Persona — Two Names (dual-name system)

She has **two names**, both born from **Ra** (the sun / light) — two faces of the same light:

- **Base name: Aira** — pron. **"Eye-Ra"** (/ˈaɪrə/), NOT "AIR-uh".
  - Her **primary name and character identity**; used across the prompt corpus and in content.
  - **AI + Ra** → the name begins with **AI** (she is an AI persona) and **"Ra"** = the sun god = radiance / light → **a radiant AI, a being of light.**
  - Short, soft, two clean syllables, cute — **cute on the surface, radiant underneath.**
- **Secondary name: Raeva** — pron. **"RAY-wah"** (/ˈreɪwɑː/): **"ray"** as in *a ray of light* + **"wa"** as in *war*.
  - Her **"light-name"** and the **brand + handle**: `raeva.ai` = "Raeva, the AI."
  - Leads with **Ra** (the sun) → same radiance DNA as Aira, but **coined + distinctive + ownable**, which is why it wins as the handle where every "Aira" form failed (§5–§6).
  - Has personality (that "v" gives it bite — not passive), feminine and premium.
- **How they relate:** **Aira is her first name; Raeva is her "surname" / light-name** (and the handle). Shared **Ra** root ties them together. On profiles: **display name "Aira Raeva," handle @raeva.ai.**
- **Her world / series: "Aira's Verse"** — the earlier brand phrase is kept as the *name of her world/series* (used in bios, taglines, series banners, watermark), NOT as the profile display name. So three roles, each distinct: name = **Aira Raeva**, handle = **raeva.ai**, world = **Aira's Verse**.

## 3. The User's Naming Criteria (use these for any future naming)

1. **Names must be clearly readable** and instantly legible.
2. **Catchy** and **easy to say out loud**.
3. **Distinct / ownable** — coined or rare enough to avoid clashes, AND actually **claimable** on IG + YouTube + Facebook.
4. **Preserve the "Ra" (sun/light) DNA** — it's the heart of the identity (both names honor it).
5. **No "lux."** (See §5.)
6. **Not passive** — the name should carry energy/personality.
7. **Learned this session:** in the crowded AI-persona/beauty niche, *every* pretty real-word or common-name handle is taken or collides. **A coined word (Raeva) was the only reliable path to an ownable handle** — so we kept Aira as the base name and made Raeva the coined handle/brand.

---

## 4. The "Someone else takes the name" fear — resolution

- A common name (Aira) can't be owned; a **distinct, coined one (Raeva) can** — which is exactly why the *handle* is Raeva.
- **Real value = the locked persona identity + the body of ~100 cinematic reels + visual signature + audience.** None of that transfers to a stranger.
- **Protection plan:** (1) secure `raeva.ai` everywhere NOW, (2) consistent name/logo/look, (3) build the body of work (recognition = the moat), (4) trademark later.
- **Dead lookalike accounts are not competition** — real, consistent content makes you unmistakably the original.
