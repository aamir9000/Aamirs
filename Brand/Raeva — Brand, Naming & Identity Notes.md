## 3. The User's Naming Criteria (use these for any future naming)

1. **The name must be clearly readable** and instantly legible — no blends that chop/hide it.
2. **Catchy** and **easy to say out loud** (rolls off the tongue).
3. **Distinct / ownable** — coined or rare enough that a random account can't clash, AND actually **claimable** on IG + YouTube + Facebook.
4. **Preserve the "Ra" (sun/light) DNA** where possible — it's the heart of the identity.
5. **No "lux."** (See §5 for why.)
6. **Not passive** — the name should carry energy/personality, not read sleepy or generic.
7. **Learned this session:** in the crowded AI-persona/beauty niche, *every* pretty real-word or common-name handle (aira·*, *·verse, glow, lumi, sol, nova, seraphine, lumira, solene…) is already taken or collides. **A coined/invented word is the only reliable path to an ownable handle.**

## 4. The "Someone else takes the name" fear — resolution (for reassurance if it recurs)

- A common word/name can't be owned; a **distinct, coined brand can**. That's why we landed on **Raeva** (coined) instead of another "Aira·something."
- **Real value of the project = the locked persona identity + the body of ~100 cinematic reels + visual signature + audience.** None of that transfers to a stranger using a similar word.
- **Protection plan:** (1) secure `raeva.ai` everywhere NOW (possession ≈ ownership on social), (2) consistent name/logo/look across platforms, (3) build the body of work (recognition = the moat), (4) trademark later when it's worth it.
- **Dead lookalike accounts are not competition** — empty profiles have zero brand equity; real, consistent content makes you unmistakably the original.
