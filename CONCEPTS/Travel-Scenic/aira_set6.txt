AIRA — CINEMATIC JOURNEY / LIGHT-RIDE REELS · SET 6
========================================================================
Built for: Nano Banana Pro (start-frame stills) → Veo 3.1 (normal /
frames-to-video mode). Each reel = 3–5 SEPARATE shot-clips; the CUTS
between clips serve as the angle changes. Cut them together in the edit.

Inspiration lineage (elevated beyond, never copied): epic cinematic
skies + lens-flare rides (photoreal travel reels) · crane-reveal scale
(anime valley leaps) · dynamic angle variety + a glowing motion wake
(anime commute). Our ownable upgrade = the IRIDESCENT LIGHT-TRAIL: a
being of light leaving a luminous wake, grounded in real light physics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL RULES (apply to every shot in this set)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTITY LOCK]  SUBJECT = .  Copy face/likeness from the reference
image ONLY. Treat identity as a FIXED skeleton — bone structure, eye
shape & colour, nose, lips, skin, hairline — that NEVER changes. Animate
only soft expression muscles, gaze and head. No reshaping, no age
change, no plastic/CGI skin. One smooth eye-led beat per clip.

[RENDER MANDATE — CONTROLLED VIBRANCY]  Cinematic, filmic, ultra-
detailed, high dynamic range. Vibrancy comes from LIGHT & CONTRAST
(rim-light, speculars, bloom, bright subject vs. deeper/cooler ground) —
NOT from blanket saturation. Curated palette per concept: one dominant
hue family + 1–2 accents, anchored by neutrals/negative space. Protect
natural skin tone — never push saturation onto the face. No neon-on-neon
mud, no rainbow-everything, no blown highlights.

[THE LIGHT-TRAIL SIGNATURE]  A luminous iridescent wake (pink · lilac ·
aqua, per-concept tinted) ribbons off her motion — wheel, feet, or
trailing fabric. Grounded like a real long-exposure light-streak +
volumetric bloom + wet-ground reflection. NEVER cheap sparkles, cartoon
VFX, floaty weightless nonsense, or plastic CGI. It obeys real light:
it fades with distance, reflects on surfaces, and bends with her path.

[FIGURE-TO-GROUND]  The WORLD is a co-star. Per reel guarantee a spread:
at least ONE true establishing WIDE and ONE environment-dominant/FAR
placement (world = the larger area, subject smaller), plus balanced
mediums. Tight CU crops are the MINORITY. Build real foreground /
midground / background depth, leading lines, parallax. Never a floating
subject on an empty backdrop.

[MOTION]  Real-time natural speed. NO slow motion. Every clip starts
"already in motion" (continue an existing motion vector — no static cold
start). One continuous camera move per clip. Real physics: vehicle
dynamics, wind, weight, cast shadows, believable everything.

[ANGLE COVERAGE across the 3–5 clips]  Never repeat a framing. Rotate
through: worm's-eye low-hero · Dutch-tilt side-track · first-person POV ·
over-the-shoulder · high overhead / crane reveal · profile track · wide
establishing. Vary crop (ECU/CU/half/cowboy/full/wide), angle, and
placement (left/right/centre/near/far) every clip.

[ORIENTATION]  Each concept is tagged 9:16 (vertical reel) or 16:9
(cinematic landscape). Keep the tagged ratio for all clips in that
concept unless a shot says otherwise.

[PER-SHOT FIELD TEMPLATE]  Every IMAGE (start-frame) block specifies:
Aspect · Framing & subject position · Head & gaze · Torso/hips/weight ·
Arms→hands→fingers (R & L named) · Legs & feet (R & L named) · Hair ·
Wardrobe · Headwear (if any) · Accessories · Footwear · Hands & nails ·
Vehicle/key object (deep) · Light-trail · Environment fg/mg/bg (deep) ·
Sky (deep) · Lighting · Lens & DoF · Palette/grade · Realism · Negatives.
Every VIDEO block specifies: Aspect/Duration/FPS · Shot type/angle/camera
move · Head & gaze arc + micro-expression · Limb movement (arms/legs) ·
Hip/torso/weight motion · Hair & fabric dynamics · Wardrobe/accessory
continuity · Vehicle motion & physics · Light-trail behaviour ·
Environment motion & parallax · Sky motion · Atmosphere/FX · Lighting in
motion · Color grade · Audio · Continuity/loop · Negatives.

[STD-NEG — IMAGE]  reshaped/warped face; wrong eye colour; plastic CGI
skin; extra/melted fingers; body-shape invention; blown highlights;
blanket oversaturation; neon mud; cheap sparkles/cartoon VFX; empty or
ignored background; subject filling the whole frame; text artifacts;
watermark; distorted vehicle.

[STD-NEG — VIDEO]  slow motion; static cold-start; face warp/identity
drift; rubbery limbs; weightless floaty motion; teleporting objects;
flickering; morphing wardrobe; cartoon speed-lines; blown highlights;
duplicated subject; jelly/parallax errors.

CONCEPT 01 — "COASTAL BLUE-HOUR LIGHT RIDE"        ORIENTATION: 9:16
========================================================================
LOGLINE: . rides a sleek e-scooter along a clifftop coastal boulevard as
blue hour tips into a molten-gold horizon, trailing a luminous iridescent
wake toward the sun-star. Serene, exhilarated, cinematic.
CLIPS: 4 (cut together = one continuous ride). 
GRADE PERSONALITY: anamorphic TEAL-&-GOLD blue-hour HDR — horizontal lens-
flares, soft halation, deep cool indigo shadows, warm gold speculars.
TRAIL TINT: pearl-lilac → aqua.
GROUNDED-MAGICAL BEAT: the light-trail = a real long-exposure light-streak
+ volumetric bloom reflecting on wet tarmac (never sparkles/cartoon VFX).

------ CONTINUITY LOCKS (IDENTICAL in all 4 clips) ------
SUBJECT: .  (identity from reference ONLY; fixed skeleton; skin natural).
WARDROBE: fitted cropped iridescent moto-jacket (pearl-lilac shifting to
aqua sheen) worn open over a fine white ribbed tee; high-waist wide-leg
trousers in pale dove-grey with a soft drape; a long (≈2 m) gauzy

HAIR: dark-brown chin-length side-parted bob, swept back by wind.

ACCESSORIES: compact iridescent teardrop earrings; slim silver band on the

FOOTWEAR: pearl-white low leather sneakers, gum soles.
